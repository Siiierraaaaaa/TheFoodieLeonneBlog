Welcome to The Foodie Leonne

A little about me.

My name is Sierra Leonne, and I'm the creator behind this blog! A space where my love for food, culture, and storytelling come together. Growing up with deep roots in Black Southern culture, I developed a passion for rich, soulful flavors and the joy that comes from sharing a great meal.

Whether it's a family recipe passed down through generations or a trendy new dish taking over social media, I love exploring every bite-worthy experience.

Behind the blog

Why I Started This Blog

Food has always been a way for me to connect—with my heritage, my family, and people around me. This blog is my way of sharing that love and helping others find joy in cooking, eating, and discovering new flavors.

Whether you're a home cook, a fellow foodie, or just looking for your next restaurant visit, I hope you find inspiration here.
