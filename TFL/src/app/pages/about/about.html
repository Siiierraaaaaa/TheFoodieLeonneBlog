Meet The Owner

Sierra Leonne

Welcome to The Foodie Leonne! I'm thrilled to have you here.

My name is Sierra Leonne, and I'm the creator behind this blog! A space where my love for food, culture, and storytelling come together. Growing up with deep roots in Black Southern culture, I developed a passion for rich, soulful flavors and the joy that comes from sharing a great meal. Whether it's a family recipe passed down through generations or a trendy new dish taking over social media, I love exploring every bite-worthy experience.

At The Foodie Leonne, I share:
Trendy Recipes
-Fun, creative dishes that are as delicious as they are Instagram-worthy.
Irresistible Desserts
- Because no meal is complete without a sweet ending!
Restaurant Features
- The best food spots in Atlanta and New Orleans, from hidden gems to iconic favorites.
Foodie Adventures
- My personal experiences with flavors, traditions, and must-try bites.
Why I Started This Blog

Food has always been a way for me to connect—with my heritage, my family, and people around me. This blog is my way of sharing that love and helping others find joy in cooking, eating, and discovering new flavors. Whether you're a home cook, a fellow foodie, or just looking for your next restaurant visit, I hope you find inspiration here.
