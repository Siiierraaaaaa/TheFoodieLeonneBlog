New Orleans is a city where every bite tells a story—rich with culture, flavor, and history. From spicy Creole classics to soulful Cajun comfort food, the culinary traditions of the Big Easy reflect its vibrant blend of African, French, Caribbean, and Native American influences. At the heart of this flavorful legacy is Leah Chase, the legendary “Queen of Creole Cuisine.” As the longtime chef and owner of Dooky Chase’s Restaurant, she served everyone from civil rights leaders to presidents, all while preserving and elevating the art of Southern cooking. In this featured video, Leah Chase shares her iconic recipe for gumbo, a beloved New Orleans dish that brings people together with every hearty, seasoned spoonful.
